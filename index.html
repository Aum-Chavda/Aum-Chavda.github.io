Self-Supervised Learning JEPA · From Scratch
Mini I-JEPA — Image JEPA from Scratch
Built a minimal but complete implementation of I-JEPA (Assran et al., CVPR 2023) from scratch in PyTorch. ViT-Tiny context encoder updated by backprop, EMA-frozen target encoder, and a narrow bottleneck predictor that forces generalisation without reconstruction. Trained on STL-10 (100k unlabelled) on a GTX 1650 Ti. Full production-grade OOP — ABC contracts, Protocol callbacks, O(1) DS&A patch masking, sentinel padding, vectorised torch.gather for patch selection.
